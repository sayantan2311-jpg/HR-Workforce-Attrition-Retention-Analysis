# HR-Workforce-Attrition-Retention-Analysis
End-to-end HR analytics capstone using Power BI, Python and SQL to analyse employee attrition, promotions and retention. Includes a 20-page interactive dashboard, Python EDA and 20 SQL business scenarios.

🛠️ Tools & Skills
Area	Details
Power BI	Data modelling, DAX measures, KPI cards, slicers, interactive dashboards
Python	Exploratory Data Analysis (univariate, bivariate, multivariate), correlation heatmaps
SQL	Window functions (LAG), CTEs, joins, cohort retention, data-quality checks, stored procedures
Presentation	Business insight and conclusion for every analysis question.

📈 Power BI Dashboard 

A multi-page dashboard built around KPIs such as Total Employees, Employees Left, Attrition Rate, Average Income, Average Tenure and Promotion Rate. Pages cover:

Exit data to retention strategy (overview)
Salary bands vs. attrition
Work-life balance and overtime impact
Remote work effectiveness
Promotion strategy and leadership development
Commuting distance, company reputation and employee recognition
Early employee exits, diversity and retention review
Cost of attrition estimation and an executive HR dashboard
A 12-month workforce retention strategy

🐍 Python Analysis

Business questions answered with visualisations, each with an insight and a conclusion:

Average age, monthly income by job role, and salary by gender
Total attrition and average work-life balance score
Does overtime increase attrition?
Does monthly income increase with job level?
Which job roles have the highest attrition?
Does distance from home or remote work affect attrition?
Which factors correlate most strongly with attrition and promotions?
Do leadership opportunities, company reputation and performance ratings relate to promotions, retention and pay?

🗄️ SQL Analysis

Includes monthly revenue tracking with drop detection, customer lifetime value, product pairs bought together (support, confidence, lift), SaaS churn features, inventory snapshots, SKU performance by region, duplicate record detection, A/B test analysis, retention cohort matrix, ETL batch audit, fraud rule detection, incremental dashboard summaries, month-end adjustments with audit logging, and PII anonymisation.

Where the dataset did not contain the fields a scenario needed, the scenario was adapted and the assumption is documented in the analysis.

💡 Key Takeaways
Overtime shows a stronger relationship with attrition than monthly income, which suggests workload matters more than pay alone
Higher job levels are associated with higher income
Performance is an important factor in promotion decisions
Distance from home is not the only reason employees leave, so HR should look at several factors together
Flexible and remote work options may help reduce attrition
